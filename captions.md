Samain marked the end of summer in Gaelic Ireland. The Gaulish Coligny calendar, inscribed in the second century AD, opens its year with the month Samonios. Irish literature fixed its terrors on this night — gates between worlds unlocked, the dead permitted passage. In Echtra Nerai, Nera carries a hanged man to drink, then follows a spectral host into the hill. Few return from that tale unchanged. Three weeks remain before the thirty-first. ## Friday — The Demon Catalogue

File: `2026-10-09-5.mp4` · mood: bells · archive: alloccult.com/ceremonial-magic/goetia-demonology

Johann Weyer defended accused witches in 1563. Many, he wrote in De praestigiis daemonum, were ill or deceived — not guilty of congress with devils. Jean Bodin answered him in 1580, calling Weyer a sorcerer and demanding the fire for witches. Weyer's 1577 edition carried an appendix: Pseudomonarchia Daemonum. Sixty-nine spirits, catalogued to expose the folly of conjurers. Instead, it became a primary source for the Goetia of Solomon, which lists seventy-two. The full lineage is at alloccult.com

## Saturday — The Alphabet of Honorius

File: `2026-10-09-6.mp4` · mood: ritual · archive: alloccult.com/symbols-sigils/sacred-languages

The Theban alphabet appeared in print in 1518, in Trithemius's Polygraphia. He attributed it to one Honorius of Thebes, transmitted through Pietro d'Abano. No manuscript evidence confirms Honorius wrote a word of it. Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa carried it into De Occulta Philosophia in 1533. Modern witchcraft adopted it as a sacred cipher.
