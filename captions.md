## Tuesday — The Six-Month Operation  [APPROVED]

File: `2026-10-19-2.mp4` · mood: ritual · alloccult.com/grimoires-sacred-texts/classical-grimoires

The Book of Abramelin claims to date from 1458.
S. L. MacGregor Mathers translated a French copy in 1898, from the Bibliotheque de l'Arsenal in Paris.
It prescribes six months of prayer and withdrawal.
German manuscripts, edited later, demand eighteen.
Then the angel. Then the spirits.
In 1899 Crowley bought Boleskine House to attempt it.
By 1900 he had left for Paris.

## Saturday — Sixty-Nine Hours on a Snakeskin  [APPROVED]

File: `2026-10-19-6.mp4` · mood: ritual · alloccult.com/witchcraft-folk-magic/hoodoo-rootwork

In late-1920s New Orleans, she went looking for hoodoo doctors.
Luke Turner claimed Marie Laveau as his kin.
Her initiation: sixty-nine hours face down, her navel touching a snakeskin, without food.
She came out with a name: the Rain-Bringer.
She published it all in Mules and Men, 1935.
