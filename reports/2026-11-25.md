# ALLOCCULT — Competitor Intel & Content Plan (2026-11-25)

## 1. Summary

* Carousels dominate organic reach, generating more than double the median engagement of Reels.
* Strong ideological conviction and deconstruction of modern dogmas drive intense comment debate.
* Physical occult relics and primary texts outperform generic digital artwork.
* Peak engagement clusters between 14:00 and 20:00 UTC, favoring late-afternoon revelation over morning broadcasts.

## 2. What's Working

### Ideological Contention and Deconstruction
Taking uncompromising stances on occult history fractures passivity. Followers demand conviction.
* Hook: *"In case anyone has forgotten… Witchcraft is political."*
* Hook: *"More witchy things that I stopped believing because they did not serve me or my practice at all."*
* Medium-length expository captions. Strong prompts asking followers to confess abandoned beliefs.
* Examples: [Darkly Divine Crystals](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfUrKZckfLY/), [Darkly Divine Crystals](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfALkD-EYgf/)

### Archival Material and Direct Lineage
Documenting physical artifacts, named practitioners, and verifiable dates triggers elite engagement.
* Hook: *"We are so excited to be able to share this stunning piece of carving by Bel Bucca, with our Patrons tomorrow."*
* Hook: *"After the name change of the museum, we have finally installed the new sign above the museum."*
* Short, authoritative provenance notes. Calls to action direct followers to patronage or deeper study.
* Examples: [Museum of Witchcraft and Magic](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfsSJEkAHGT/), [Museum of Magic Fortune Witchcraft](https://www.instagram.com/p/Df-NhsRCDZh/)

### Astrological Ingress as Forbidden Gateways
Timing specific historical or esoteric lore to lunar and planetary thresholds commands attention.
* Hook: *"NEW MOON IN SCORPIO ♏️🌑 The second New Moon of fall arrives tonight..."*
* Exhaustive captions tracking exact astrological timing, planetary dignities, and psychological integration.
* Example: [The Hoodwitch](https://www.instagram.com/p/DffhzcPmLH1/)
